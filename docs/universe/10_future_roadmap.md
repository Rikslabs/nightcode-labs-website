# NightCode Universe — Future Roadmap

## Long-term premise

The campus expands by accretion, not replacement. Its original geography, landmarks, material grammar, and emotional sequence remain valid as new capabilities arrive. Growth should make the first campus feel more complete, not obsolete.

## Decade framework

### Years 1–2: Establishment

Complete the central campus: Visitor Center, Headquarters, primary route, Research, Studio, Games, and Energy Core. Establish the visual and environmental baseline before increasing complexity.

### Years 3–4: Operational depth

Expose carefully selected infrastructure, deepen building interiors, and connect JARVIS Tower to the campus circulation and signal network. Add service behavior and recurring environmental cycles.

### Years 5–6: Vertical and civic growth

Extend Headquarters and JARVIS Tower within their existing silhouettes. Add public observation, shared forums, and research-to-studio connections. Preserve the central civic route.

### Years 7–8: First island activation

Activate one Future Island with a clear new capability. Connect it through the Energy Core and a legible transfer route. The island must be visible from the original campus before it is fully accessible.

### Years 9–10: Networked campus

Add further islands only where the prior island proves the need for expansion. Let each island contribute a distinct function while sharing common infrastructure, light logic, and return routes.

## Expansion invariants

- Headquarters remains the civic center.
- JARVIS Tower remains the vertical reference.
- Energy Core remains the infrastructural origin.
- Visitor Center remains the public threshold.
- The original route remains navigable and understandable.
- New districts add capability, not replacement aesthetics.
- New materials are introduced sparingly and documented before use.

## Expansion decision sequence

```text
need identified → existing district tested → route established
→ infrastructure attached → landmark defined → atmosphere tuned
→ public threshold designed → return path confirmed
```

If a proposed expansion requires redesigning the universe’s foundational rules, it is not an expansion; it is a separate world and must be rejected or formally re-scoped.
