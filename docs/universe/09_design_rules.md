# NightCode Universe — Immutable Design Rules

These rules are normative. A future feature, district, asset, or interaction may extend the universe only if it remains compatible with them.

1. Architecture is navigation.
2. Motion has purpose.
3. Nature and engineering coexist.
4. No generic landing pages.
5. No fake futuristic elements.
6. Reality before spectacle.
7. Every district has a purpose, a story, and a threshold.
8. Every landmark is legible at distance and meaningful at approach.
9. Materials must communicate weight, age, and function.
10. Light follows source, activity, safety, or orientation.
11. The campus remains one world; districts do not become disconnected scenes.
12. Mystery may delay explanation, but may not destroy orientation.
13. Public routes are generous; restricted routes are intentional.
14. Scale transitions must include a human reference.
15. Systems shown in the world must imply a credible cause.
16. Silence and stillness are designed states.
17. New spectacle requires an equally strong reason to exist.
18. Future expansion inherits the universe before it adds vocabulary.

## Review test

Before approval, ask: Does this improve orientation, purpose, atmosphere, story, or discovery? If it does none of these, it does not belong in the universe.
