# NightCode Universe — Motion Language

## Motion principles

Motion communicates scale, activity, causality, and invitation. It is never used to decorate an otherwise static surface.

## Camera movement

Camera movement is deliberate, smooth, and spatially motivated. Large moves reveal relationships; small moves reveal texture or activity. Avoid abrupt acceleration, arbitrary spins, and movement that obscures the route.

## Animation principles

- Establish cause before effect where possible.
- Prefer continuous cycles with small variation over obvious loops.
- Let heavy objects move slowly and light signals move quickly.
- Use stillness as a meaningful state.
- Preserve a stable reference during transitions.

## Drone behaviour

Drones are utility actors: surveying, carrying, guiding, inspecting, or maintaining. They follow routes, pause at work, and yield to important views. They do not swarm, perform, or compete with the visitor.

## Bridge behaviour

Bridges are connectors first. Their motion may indicate traffic, energy, or state change, but they never surprise a visitor at a threshold. A bridge should announce its destination and settle before crossing.

## Environmental motion

Water flows downhill. Vegetation responds subtly to wind. Clouds travel slowly across the sky. Energy pulses along credible paths. Lighting changes propagate from sources rather than blinking independently.

## Interaction timing

Orientation changes are immediate enough to preserve agency. Reveals and environmental responses are measured, allowing cause and effect to be perceived. Important transitions include a pause or settling period.

## Motion rules

1. Motion has purpose.
2. Motion must preserve orientation.
3. Motion must have a readable source or reason.
4. Motion must not imply impossible weight without narrative justification.
5. Every animated system needs a resting state.
