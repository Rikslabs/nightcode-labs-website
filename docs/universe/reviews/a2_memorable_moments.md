# NightCode Arrival — A2 Memorable Moments

## Scope

This pass stages five places inside the existing arrival corridor. It adds no districts, products, interactions, or navigation systems.

## Moment 1 — Arrival Bridge

The reveal phase begins from a low, long bridge suspended above the cloud field. Headquarters is deliberately obscured by haze and the bridge’s near perspective. Sparse edge lighting and distant supports imply wind, height, and engineered calm. The destination is present but not yet available as a complete image.

**Emotion:** Curiosity.

## Moment 2 — The Reveal

During the approach phase, elevation and cloud movement open the view toward Headquarters. The building’s central spine and broad wings become legible before secondary detail. JARVIS Tower appears later as a vertical reference, preserving Headquarters as the first architectural recognition.

**Emotion:** Wonder.

## Moment 3 — Reflection Court

The plaza phase holds on a civic terrace with a shallow reflection pool, engineered stone, planting, low seating-like edges, and a restrained civic bridge. Nothing performs. The pause is created by proportion, water, and the relationship between the terrace and Headquarters entrance.

**Emotion:** Presence.

## Moment 4 — Headquarters Threshold

The Grand Atrium is the endpoint. The threshold uses compression and release: dark exterior mass gives way to columns, a broad floor, a high ceiling, and one quiet architectural light. The visitor arrives through space and material, not a transition effect.

**Emotion:** Belonging.

## Moment 5 — Cloud Edge

Before the threshold, the plaza frame retains a cloud-edge overlook. It lets the visitor look back across the cloud ocean toward bridges, distant islands, JARVIS Tower, Energy Core, and Headquarters. The view is placed as a calm secondary frame, not a new destination.

**Emotion:** Perspective.

## Camera and pacing

```text
black threshold → bridge held in haze → Headquarters earned by approach
→ reflection court pause → cloud-edge look-back → Grand Atrium
```

The timed sequence is intentionally slower: 5.2 seconds black, 16 seconds reveal, 42 seconds approach, and 16 seconds plaza before the static atrium endpoint.

## Performance

All moments use existing scene phases and lightweight layered surfaces. No new JavaScript animation loop, dependency, renderer, or interactive state was introduced.

## Limitations

The bridge, geology, pool, and cloud-edge view are still stylized approximations. Their spatial relationships should receive screenshot-based art direction before any future interior work.
