# NightCode Campus — Landmark Hierarchy

## Tier 1 — Geographic anchors

### Headquarters

The broad civic mass and emotional center. It receives the most consistent public framing and remains visible from the principal southern approach.

### Energy Core

The infrastructural center. Its waterfall, basin, and concentrated light explain the campus’s activity and provide a grounded counterpoint to the tower.

### JARVIS Tower

The vertical and operational reference. Its crown is the most distant recognizable object and stabilizes orientation across districts.

## Tier 2 — District identifiers

### Research Plateau

Recognizable through its elevated, quiet terrace and observatory-like working volumes.

### Games Basin

Recognizable through open play fields, active bridges, and a more kinetic light condition.

### Studio Isle

Recognizable through its long causeway, production hangar, and warm working glow.

## Tier 3 — Local orientation markers

Visitor Harbor, gardens, drone ports, observation decks, water terraces, and bridge landings help visitors orient within a district. They may be more detailed and intimate, but must not compete with Tier 1 silhouettes in long views.

## Visual priority

```text
distance:   JARVIS crown → Headquarters mass → Energy Core light
campus:     district silhouette → bridge → threshold
human:      landing court → garden → material junction → activity
```

New landmarks must declare their tier before they are placed. A Tier 3 object cannot accidentally become a skyline anchor.
