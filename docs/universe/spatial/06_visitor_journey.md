# NightCode Campus — First Visitor Journey

## Arrival

The visitor approaches across open water toward Visitor Harbor. The low southern datum makes the campus rise in layers: harbor, Visitor Center, Headquarters, and finally the JARVIS crown. The first understanding is geographic, not informational.

## Observation

At the Arrival Platform, the visitor pauses before entering. The harbor, central route, and northern skyline are visible together. Water movement and distant service activity establish that the campus is operating.

## Curiosity

The Visitor Center frames the first question rather than answering every question. The visitor notices the Core glow below Headquarters and sees the Research and Studio routes diverge beyond the civic axis.

## Approach

The Civic Bridge becomes a gradual ascent. Each landing reveals more of Headquarters while maintaining the tower as a fixed reference. The visitor feels the campus’s scale through distance and elevation.

## Entry

Headquarters is entered through a compressed threshold that opens into the central forum. Stone and concrete give way to glass, activity, and vertical light. The change is spatial and emotional: outsider becomes participant.

## Transformation

From the forum, the visitor chooses a district. Research narrows and quiets the pace; Studio warms and becomes tactile; Games opens and accelerates; Energy Core deepens the atmosphere. Each transformation is architectural, not interface-driven.

## Exploration

Secondary routes reveal gardens, observation decks, bridges, and operational traces. The visitor is rewarded for looking around, but the Headquarters Plaza and JARVIS Tower remain recoverable references.

## Return

The route returns through a high overlook or the Civic Bridge. The visitor sees the campus as a coherent whole and recognizes the same harbor that began the journey. Departure leaves a future island or distant route unresolved but credible.
