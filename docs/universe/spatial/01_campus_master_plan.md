# NightCode Campus — Master Plan

## Campus philosophy

The campus is a vertical working landscape rather than a collection of buildings. Public arrival begins at the low southern edge, shared civic life occupies the central terraces, research and production rise along the northern slopes, and infrastructure remains visibly connected beneath them. The arrangement creates a readable ascent from welcome to invention to energy.

The plan is intentionally incomplete at its outer edges. The existing campus is a durable first settlement inside a larger geography.

## Conceptual dimensions

The initial landform is approximately 2.4 km east–west by 1.8 km north–south, measured as a navigable campus rather than a property boundary. The principal public journey covers approximately 900 m from Visitor Harbor to the Energy Core overlook. Dimensions are relational and may be rescaled while preserving sequence, proportion, and sightlines.

## Primary districts

Headquarters is central because it is the civic and organizational heart. Visitor Center and Visitor Harbor occupy the south because approach from open water provides the clearest orientation and the gentlest first elevation. Energy Core occupies the central low basin beneath the working districts so energy, water, and service routes can distribute outward and downward.

Research is on the north plateau for quiet, distance, and elevated observation. Studio Isle sits west of the Core because production benefits from separation, daylight, and a direct relationship to Research. Games occupies the east basin because its open volumes and active routes can absorb movement without disturbing the focused north edge. JARVIS Tower rises northeast as the campus’s vertical reference and intelligence landmark.

## Secondary districts

Secondary spaces include gardens, service yards, drone ports, observation decks, maintenance paths, water terraces, and sheltered civic courts. They occupy the interstitial land between primary districts and make the campus feel inhabited without competing with the primary silhouettes.

## Future expansion zones

The west horizon beyond Studio Isle is reserved for production and education. The east horizon beyond Games Basin is reserved for robotics and experimental interaction. The northern shelf beyond Research is reserved for an AI campus. Subsurface and offshore zones around the Energy Core are reserved for cloud infrastructure and unknown products.

## Landmark hierarchy

Tier 1 landmarks establish geography: Headquarters, Energy Core, and JARVIS Tower. Tier 2 landmarks identify working districts: Research Plateau, Studio Isle, and Games Basin. Tier 3 landmarks support local orientation: Visitor Harbor, gardens, drone ports, and observation decks.
