# NightCode Campus — Bridge Network

Every bridge performs circulation, orientation, service, or emergency work. Bridges are named infrastructure, not decorative connectors.

## Civic Bridge

**Purpose:** Main public ascent from Visitor Center to Headquarters Plaza.

**Visual language:** Broad, shallow, gently rising civic promenade with framed views.

**Materials:** Stone deck, concrete ribs, glass wind screens, titanium handrails.

**Lighting:** Warm low-level guidance at the walking edge; no overhead glare.

**Traffic:** Visitors, staff, light service vehicles.

**Connects:** Visitor Center and Headquarters.

## Research Bridge

**Purpose:** Direct connection between Headquarters and Research Plateau.

**Visual language:** Narrower elevated crossing with deliberate moments of pause and observation.

**Materials:** Titanium structure, dark glass, concrete landing courts.

**Lighting:** Cool linear signals at joints and landing points.

**Traffic:** Researchers, staff, small maintenance units.

**Connects:** Headquarters and Research.

## Studio Causeway

**Purpose:** Moves people and equipment between Energy Core logistics and Studio Isle.

**Visual language:** Low, heavy causeway that reads as a production route rather than a scenic walk.

**Materials:** Concrete, stone ballast, protected metal service rails.

**Lighting:** Functional amber markers, stronger at loading points.

**Traffic:** Studio crews, equipment, maintenance, emergency access.

**Connects:** Studio and Energy Core.

## Games Bridge

**Purpose:** Connects Headquarters Plaza to the active Games Basin.

**Visual language:** Slightly arced pedestrian bridge with visible movement and playfield views.

**Materials:** Titanium frame, resilient composite walking surface, controlled colored glass.

**Lighting:** Variable but bounded light that responds to activity in the basin.

**Traffic:** Visitors, players, staff, event circulation.

**Connects:** Headquarters and Games.

## Intelligence Spine

**Purpose:** Links Headquarters, Energy Core, and JARVIS Tower operations.

**Visual language:** Protected vertical and diagonal infrastructure, more service spine than promenade.

**Materials:** Titanium, concrete anchors, dark transparent shielding.

**Lighting:** Pulsed operational light that indicates active systems.

**Traffic:** Authorized staff, maintenance, emergency teams.

**Connects:** Headquarters, Energy Core, and JARVIS Tower.

## Emergency High Route

**Purpose:** Redundant all-weather movement between the Core, Headquarters, and upper districts.

**Visual language:** Robust, partially enclosed, and visually subordinate to public bridges.

**Materials:** Concrete, structural titanium, protected glass.

**Lighting:** Continuous safety illumination with distinct emergency beacons.

**Traffic:** Emergency and maintenance only.

**Connects:** Energy Core, Headquarters, Research, Studio, and Games.
