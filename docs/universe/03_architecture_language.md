# NightCode Universe — Architecture Language

## Architectural style

The style is restrained future-institutional: observatory, laboratory, civic campus, and remote outpost combined. It values durable geometry, visible structure, generous voids, and precise junctions. Futurity is communicated through integration and behavior, not ornamental complexity.

## Proportions

- Primary masses use simple, readable geometries with a strong horizontal or vertical datum.
- Large public spaces are balanced by small service and observation spaces.
- Openings are deep enough to read as structure, not surface graphics.
- Repetition is used for rhythm; variation is reserved for orientation and significance.
- Every landmark has a silhouette legible at distance and a detail legible at approach.

## Scale

The campus operates across three scales:

```text
regional: horizon, islands, tower silhouette
campus: districts, routes, bridges, plazas
human: thresholds, windows, vegetation, tools, light
```

Transitions between scales must be gradual. A visitor should never feel that a building is a miniature or a district is an abstract diagram.

## Material language

Glass provides depth and observation. Titanium provides precision and protection. Concrete provides mass and civic permanence. Stone provides geological continuity. Water, vegetation, and energy provide change.

Junctions are honest: supports meet surfaces, light reveals edges, and transparent volumes disclose enough of their structure to remain credible.

## Design rules

- Prefer one strong mass over many competing objects.
- Use voids as active spatial elements.
- Let circulation determine façades and openings.
- Reserve luminous accents for systems, thresholds, and active states.
- Make maintenance, weathering, and gravity perceptible where appropriate.
- Distinguish public, operational, and restricted areas through access and atmosphere.

## Prohibited styles

- Generic glass-box corporate campuses.
- Unmotivated cyberpunk neon, holographic clutter, or floating ornaments.
- Medieval, retro-futurist, or fantasy motifs without a defined district reason.
- Hyper-organic blobs that erase structure and orientation.
- Monumental forms that cannot be approached, entered, or understood.
- Decorative machinery with no operational implication.
