# NightCode Universe — Navigation

## Navigation philosophy

Navigation is a spatial conversation, not a menu exercise. The world should answer three questions continuously: Where am I? What matters here? What can I approach next?

## Journey

```text
arrival → exploration → discovery → approach → building entry
        → transformation → return
```

### Arrival

Begin with a stable, elevated or open view that establishes the campus, its central landmark, and the current route. Arrival must reduce uncertainty before introducing detail.

### Exploration

Provide a legible primary route and visible alternatives. Exploration is self-directed; the world may invite without forcing.

### Discovery

Secondary details, activity, changing light, and distant structures reward attention. Discovery should clarify the institution rather than function as collectible decoration.

### Approach

As a destination nears, its material, sound implication, lighting, and scale become more specific. The approach is part of the destination.

### Building entry

Entry is a threshold event. The exterior world recedes, the building’s dominant purpose becomes legible, and the visitor receives a new spatial frame.

### Transformation

Each district may alter orientation, pace, light, and available depth. Transformation must feel like crossing into a real operating condition.

### Return

Routes back to the campus should restore the larger geography. A visitor can leave a building knowing where they are in relation to the whole.
