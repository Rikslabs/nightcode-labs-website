# NightCode Universe — Master Plan

## Universe

The universe is a bounded but expandable archipelago-campus. The initial campus occupies a central landmass surrounded by water, with the horizon reserved for future territory. The full world is visible enough to create orientation, while detail is concentrated along the current route.

## Sky Layer

The sky is the uppermost spatial reference and the largest indicator of time, weather, and scale. It carries no permanent advertising, interface chrome, or arbitrary decoration. Its changes are gradual and coherent with the campus below.

## Campus Layer

The campus is the inhabitable middle layer: terrain, districts, buildings, bridges, paths, vegetation, water, and landmarks. It is organized around a central civic route connecting arrival to the principal districts.

## Infrastructure Layer

Infrastructure is the hidden-visible system that makes the world believable. Energy lines, service routes, water movement, signal beacons, landing points, and structural connections may be seen where they improve understanding. They are never exposed merely to imply complexity.

## Buildings

Buildings are vertical anchors and narrative thresholds. Each has one dominant silhouette, one recognizable material emphasis, and one clear relationship to its district. Buildings are not cards, portals, or isolated monuments; they are destinations with an approach, an entry condition, and an interior atmosphere.

## District relationships

```text
                 [JARVIS Tower]
                       │
[Research] ─── [Headquarters] ─── [Studio]
       \           │          /
        \     [Visitor Center]
                 │
             [Games]
                 │
            [Energy Core]
                 │
          horizon / future islands
```

Headquarters is the civic center. JARVIS Tower provides vertical orientation and intelligence. Research and Studio form the invention axis. Games provides play and testing. Visitor Center is the public threshold. Energy Core is the deep infrastructural anchor. Future Islands extend the campus without changing these relationships.

## Future expansion

Expansion proceeds outward from existing infrastructure and outward-facing edges. New districts must attach to an existing route, inherit a known landmark language, and introduce one new capability or mood. The central campus remains the canonical orientation point.
